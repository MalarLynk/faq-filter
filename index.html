Warranty
Warranty How long does it take for the warranty pre-approval work to be established? Within a working day (M-F).
Warranty Any general list of the warranty claim list of a VIN? Not right now, currently we only can view the work list that was noted in Work Order App.
Warranty If the parts order got a warranty rejection, what do we need to do next? Conduct work order cancellation first after receiving warranty rejection.

Visit Video Vault - Warranty Claim in Work Order App & Jira video course if you need to learn more.

Do inform the customer about the warranty claim rejection, so the customer is aware of self-purchasing the service and parts from your workshop.
Warranty When operations (including parts) are added to a work order, we have to wait for approval by Lynk&Co. This is affecting lead times for repairs. However, when we order parts ourselves, after approval by Lynk we are again sent the items, which are already ordered manually. So we have double orders. Regarding Warranty parts ordering: In the Work Order App in Codiac, when adding an operation you can select and remove unnecessary parts before ordering.
Warranty Why do I get RC 16? RC16 means other information missing, incorrect or insufficient. In practice, the claim does not contain enough correct information to verify symptom, diagnosis, root cause or repair.
Warranty Is a long comment enough? No. The important point is whether the technical story is complete and clear.
Warranty Why do you ask for DTCs? DTCs help verify diagnostics, support trend analysis and connect symptom to technical cause when relevant.
Warranty Do all claims need photos or video? No, only when they help show the fault. But the clearer the evidence, the easier and faster the claim is to assess – when in doubt, add the photo or the short video.
Warranty Why do you need measurements? Measurements help distinguish manufacturing defects from wear, external influence or incorrect diagnosis.
Warranty Symptom vs diagnosis? – TS number Symptom is what the customer experiences. Diagnosis is how the workshop confirms the cause.
Warranty Can a claim be approved without a DTC? Yes, depending on the issue. If no DTC exists, other evidence or explanation may be required.
Warranty Why return instead of calling? The decision process must be traceable in the claim for audit and quality work.
Warranty What does No Prior Approval Part mean? The part or repair required approval before the repair or claim step.
Warranty How long should replaced parts be stored? Replaced components should be stored for 28 days unless component return is requested.
Warranty After 120 minutes of diagnosis? Create a Workshop Support case and summarize findings if root cause is still not identified.
Warranty Why do you need more than what is needed for payment? - bilar utanför garantin. Claim data supports quality improvement, supplier recovery and technical analysis.
Warranty Is a returned claim a rejection? No. It usually means Warranty needs more or corrected information before final assessment.
Warranty Can a rejected claim be appealed? A rejected claim cannot be reactivated. If new or stronger technical evidence supports a covered defect, submit a new claim with that evidence included.
Warranty Most common avoidable issue? Missing, incorrect or insufficient information is the largest driver in the supplied overviews.
Warranty How can we perform a self-check before submitting a warranty claim? Use the workflow self-check:

Complaint → Diagnosis → Root cause → Repair → Verification → Required evidence
SBI Self billing
SBI Self billing I cannot see my SBI Invoice 1) Is the warranty claim approved?
2) Is the work order in Codiac completed?
3) Please check your spam mailbox. The sender is SAP@Lynkco.com
SBI Self billing When can I expect the SBI invoice to arrive? The SBI invoice is sent automatically within 8 hours after the work order in Codiac is completed.

If data is missing from our side, the invoice will be released within 1-5 days.

If you haven't received it on Day 5, please create a JIRA Warranty ticket. Include the VIN and Codiac Work Order ID.

You can expect a response within 1 working day.
SBI Self billing I received approval for additional labor time after my work order was already approved. Will it automatically be included in my SBI? No. Additional approved time must be added as a new operation through the correct Codiac workflow.

If added correctly via a new warranty submission, it will be processed on a separate SBI.

Additional time added directly to an already approved work order will not be included in the SBI.
SBI Self billing Are Quality Bulletins (QB's) included in SBI? No. Please continue sending debit invoices for QB work.
SBI Self billing What is the SBI invoice made up of? Agreed warranty labor rates, consumables/liquids, and quantity and value of parts based on warranty parts reimbursement.
SBI Self billing How is the part price on the SBI invoice calculated? Parts prices follow the pricing of the month when the work order is closed.

Example: A work order closed in July uses July pricing.
SBI Self billing Assigned Codiac work order before April 30th - Will an SBI be generated? No. Please submit invoices according to the old routine.
SBI Self billing What if inventory price differs from claim month? Price differences between months may occur.

Currently, no compensation process is in place. Cases will be reviewed individually if raised.

Technical journal
Technical journal Why is the TJ cannot be linked to the CSC? We hope that we will get the TJs linked to the CSC, but currently it does not work that way.
Technical journal Can you show me again how to get to the TJs reports? There are several ways to access Technical Journals (TJs) in Codiac:

• Go to Operations and search for the TJ number.

• Go to Administration > Publications and search for the TJ.

• In Toolbox, click Technical Journals to open the TJ library.

• From a Work Order, select your workshop, open the vehicle, and click Technical Journals. This will display all TJs available for that specific vehicle.

All methods lead to the same Technical Journals page.
Technical journal If there is a TJ related to Fault Code, could there be a frame in notification with a different color that we can see directly of the existing node? If you encounter this issue, contact Workshop Support and submit an Operation Improvement for the relevant operation.

Clearly describe the improvement you suggest, including any missing or unclear steps. If possible, attach pictures to highlight the specific issue.

Workshop Support will review the feedback and assess whether the operation or method needs to be updated. Your feedback helps improve our processes for everyone.
Technical journal How to search for TJ? Catalogs > Operation > in the search bar, search for TJ. This is the same for all languages because it is based on the abbreviation.
Technical journal Is it possible to filter TJs by complains? No
